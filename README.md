## 💡 Key Takeaways

1. **Narrow AI Dominance:** 19 out of 20 current everyday applications are driven by Narrow AI (Artificial Narrow Intelligence), which excels at specific, single-domain tasks[cite: 2].
2. **Artificial General Intelligence (AGI):** Currently represented primarily by research models aiming toward human-level reasoning across flexible contexts[cite: 2].
3. **Cross-Industry Impact:** Artificial intelligence enhances everyday security (Face ID), safety (Medical X-Rays), convenience (Google Maps), and productivity (ChatGPT, Grammarly)[cite: 2].
